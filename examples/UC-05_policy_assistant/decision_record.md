### Gate 1: 2027-04-11. Decision: **Stop**

| Trigger | Stop or rework if | Measured | Reached? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time to find an answer with the assistant | time saved at or below 7 minutes per transaction (handling time at or above 8 minutes) | 8 minutes saved (handling time 7 minutes) | no |
| Adoption after the ramp-up | at or below 35% | 30% | **yes** |

- 3-year NPV re-run on the measured values: **-€65,576**.

Adoption trigger reached: only 30% of the pilot group's policy questions went through the assistant in month 3 (stop or rework at 35% or less). Users who used it saved time (8 minutes per question), but most kept asking colleagues or the help desk, mainly because answers on safety-relevant topics only return a link. The re-run on the measured values is negative, and the sponsor did not want to relax the safety-topic rule to raise usage. Decision: stop. The cleaned policy library and the 100 reference questions are kept for a later intake.
