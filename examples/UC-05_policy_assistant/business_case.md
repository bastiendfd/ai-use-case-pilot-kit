## 5. Downside and review gate

## 6. Counting rules applied

- A benefit counts only once. If the same saved hours or avoided errors appear in another use case, count them in one case only.
- Hours saved are not cash. They count as cash only if they are redeployed to valued work or a cost is avoided (overtime, temporary staff, a planned hire). The share is an explicit input; the rest is shown as 'not counted'.
- Benefits ramp up with adoption. Nothing starts at 100% on day one.
- Run costs are part of the case: licences, hosting, monitoring, support, retraining.
- Scenarios change only the three most uncertain inputs. All other inputs stay at their base value.
- Every input has a source or reasoning and a confidence level in the assumptions log.
- A negative scenario is a decision input, not a footnote. If the 3-year NPV is negative in any scenario, the case can only be approved with conditions: a review gate 3 months after go-live, and stop or rework if adoption or time saved fall to their pessimistic values.
